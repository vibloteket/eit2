# Favicon and logo concepts

These are retained as visual references for the future Eit 2 logo and overall
graphic style. They are concepts, not a final brand system.

- `original-e.svg` — the first simple green E favicon.
- `variant-sheet.svg` — six playful falling-block directions (A–F), including
  the selected **C: Three pieces form an E**.

Variant C became the active favicon on 2026-08-26. Keep the other concepts when
revisiting the logo, colours, block treatment and playful art direction.
